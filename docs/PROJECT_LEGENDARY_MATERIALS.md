# Project Legendary: material revision

Physical references and Pokémon lore inform original artwork; these are not physical simulations or copied game assets.

## dialga

- Dialga is associated with time.
- Dialga's lore links its existence to time's flow.

**Motion:** Two detailed chronometer faces use distinct hour, minute and exaggerated seconds-hand movement. One set runs more slowly than the other, both stop and reverse in a repeatable sequence. Separate glass hourglasses visually transfer sand from one chamber to the other and back. The hour and minute hands preserve their relative 12:1 angular progression; the seconds hand is an artistic, slowed depiction rather than a functioning clock. Clock rates are not a quantitative relativity simulation. The face includes a lunar inset, referencing astronomical timekeeping.

**Research:** [NIST: celestial, flow and mechanical clocks](https://www.nist.gov/publications/first-series-evolution-time-measurement-celestial-flow-and-mechanical-clocks); [NIST: atomic clocks and oscillator-based timekeeping](https://www.nist.gov/atomic-clocks/how-do-atomic-clocks-work); [NIST: measured gravitational time dilation](https://www.nist.gov/education/more-graphics-about-einsteins-general-relativity-and-your-age); [Einstein Online: light clocks and special relativity](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/spotlight/light-clocks-time-dilation/); [NASA: lunar phases](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/); [thermodynamic arrow of time](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.94.022135); [official Dialga Origin Forme lore](https://legends.arceus.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon/dialga/). Reversal of time is fictional Pokémon behavior, not observed physics.

**Still:** Legible clock faces with frozen hands and an upper-filled hourglass.

- [Dialga Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/0483)
- [Official Sinnoh story](https://diamondpearl.pokemon.com/en-us/story/)

## palkia

- Palkia is associated with space.
- Palkia is a legendary Pokémon of Sinnoh.

**Motion:** Original dense star and spiral-galaxy textures drift within soft violet and rose nebula clouds. A filled, irregular tear opens to reveal a second dark starfield with luminous torn edges, while curved arcs distort and brighten near the opening. The decorative lenses and portal move independently of the real interface.

**Research:** [NASA: gravitational lenses](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/science-behind-the-discoveries/hubble-gravitational-lenses/); [NASA: black hole anatomy](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/black-holes/anatomy/); [NASA: Whirlpool Galaxy](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-51/); [NASA: nebulae](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/universe-uncovered/hubble-nebulae/); [NASA: hypothetical wormholes](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/attic/cosmicopia.gsfc.nasa.gov/qa_sp_sl.html); [official Palkia Origin Forme lore](https://legends.arceus.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon/palkia/). Lensing and astronomical structures are observed; the literal portal is a fictional Spacial Rend interpretation.

**Still:** The galaxy and torn space remain visible without motion.

- [Palkia Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/0484)
- [Official Sinnoh story](https://diamondpearl.pokemon.com/en-us/story/)

## giratina

- Giratina is associated with the Distortion World.
- Giratina plays a prominent role in Platinum.

**Motion:** Irregular black-purple pools swell and settle with burgundy edges and wet highlights. Thick strands stretch out of them, while dark droplets move down and up in different layers. A spreading, irregular splatter then contracts into the pool. There are no geometric shards or orbit symbols.

**Research:** [official Giratina Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/giratina); [Giratina media depictions and shadowy tar-like form](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Giratina); [Distortion World and altered gravity](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Distortion_World); [CERN: antimatter](https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter/). The black fluid is inspired by the character's fictional shadow appearance, not a scientifically accurate depiction of antimatter, which annihilates upon contact with corresponding matter.

**Still:** A stationary dark pool and glossy strands remain; free-moving drops and splatters disappear.

- Arceus: world formation and luminous stellar material, inspired by the [Pokédex creation legend](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/arceus).
- Reshiram, Zekrom, Black and White Kyurem: shaped flame, storm clouds, branched lightning, faceted ice and vapor. [Unova legendary lore](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/strategy/unleash-unovas-legendary-elite), [Kyurem](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/kyurem), [NWS lightning](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-science-overview).

- Solgaleo and Lunala: textured solar photosphere, prominence loops, cratered lunar disc, passing shadow and stars. [NASA heliopedia](https://science.nasa.gov/reference/the-heliopedia/), [NASA moon phases](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/).
- Ultra Necrozma: shaded gold crystal and concentrated light release, [Necrozma Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/necrozma).
- Zacian and Zamazenta: polished metal sword and shield, energy wakes and outward sparks, [Behemoth Blade](https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon-galar-region/zacian/), [Behemoth Bash](https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-gb/pokemon-galar-region/zamazenta/).
- Eternatus: magenta energy clouds, luminous core, inward-moving particles and energy release. [Eternatus Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/eternatus).
- Koraidon: strong sunlight, dusty clouds and debris, inspired by its ancient form and Collision Course. [Koraidon Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/koraidon).
- Terapagos: exactly restore the pre-Project-Legendary prism/stellar and orbit/facet signatures; original generated background and artwork remain unchanged.

The generated THEME_MOTION.md remains byte-identical to the canonical compiler output. This separately maintained file documents the additional runtime signatures.

## Weather and primal refinements

- Suicune: rain driven diagonally by the existing atmospheric wind engine; a restrained blue-white palette evokes the north wind and purification lore. [Suicune](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/suicune).
- Rayquaza and Shiny Mega Rayquaza: retain the original painted sky, character art and wind engine; remove added emblem-like motifs completely. [Rayquaza](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/rayquaza).
- Koraidon: layered sandstone-like formations, fern-like vegetation and impact dust evoke ancient environments rather than symbolic sun icons. [Koraidon](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/koraidon).
- Ultra Necrozma: seven gradient-shaded crystal spires in different hues, prismatic light and subtle tip gleams, inspired by the visual variety of Z-Crystals. This is an artistic palette, not a claim that Necrozma consists of Z-Crystals. [Necrozma](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/necrozma).

## Ultra Necrozma individual jewels

Following the owner's Z-Crystal reference, replace the clustered spires with separate slanted, four-sided faceted gems. Reuse the Zygarde cell engine's seeded positions, weighted size range (18–110px), individual 7–14s grow/fade lifetimes, reduced-motion and pause behavior, and 24/10 desktop/phone density. Eighteen colors echo the supplied type-crystal palette. Jewels have original static gradient facets, without copied type emblems or additional animated children. Zygarde's layout and animation remain unchanged.
